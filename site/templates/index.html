What it does

Four planes over one sans-IO core: the same engine runs in a service, inside an ADMS, or in the simulator at a hundred times real time.

Knows the grid

A Gaussian posterior over every connection: smart meters, the transformer and LV feeders at the Ortsnetzstation, profiles for the rest — with losses, within 0.03 % of pandapower's AC flow.

FNN Hinweis 2e

Acts only when sure

A reduction starts when the estimate is determined — P99 error within 10 % of the rating — and crosses the trigger. A spoofed RTU is refused as bad data.

Ziff. 2.6, 4.1 · FNN 2c

Never below the floor

The Mindestleistung is in the type: no constructor makes a limit below it. When the floors are not enough, fms says how many kW are missing.

Ziff. 4.5

The same for everyone

Every SteuVE drawn on gets the same depth above its own floor, across the Netzbereich — and the depth comes back one 15-minute step at a time.

Ziff. 4.3

Knows what is in effect

Commanded, confirmed, failsafe, unknown: planning uses what the Steuerbox actually does, including its failsafe after a lost channel and its lapse.

FNN Hinweis 2a

Proves it

A SHA-256-chained log with a reason on every command. The customer's documentation and the monthly VNBdigital row are computed from it alone.
